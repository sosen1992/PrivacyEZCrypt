1. Information We Do Not Collect

EZCrypt is architected around absolute local privacy. Messages, notes, contacts, and cryptographic keys remain exclusively on your device:

  • Message and note content is not stored or read by us. All plain text is encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM and ECC P-256 hybrid cryptography before transmission or storage.
  • Your private encryption keys never leave your device and are never backed up to our servers.
  • Contact records, public keys, and cryptographic pairings are stored locally in secure on-device storage.
  • We do not require account registration, telephone numbers, or email sign-ins.
