Privacy Policy

EZCrypt ("the App") — Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EZCrypt ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our end-to-end encrypted messaging application EZCrypt on Android, iOS, or other supported platforms. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described below.

Developer / Publisher: SO SEN

1. Information We Do Not Collect

EZCrypt is built around privacy. Messages, contacts, and cryptographic keys are handled on your own device:

  • Message content is not stored or read by us. Messages are encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient.
  • Your private keys never leave your device and are never transmitted to us.
  • Contact records (names and public keys) are stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or phone number to use the App.

2. Information We Collect — Advertising (AdMob)

EZCrypt is monetized by displaying advertisements served by Google AdMob. We use AdMob ("third-party advertising services") to show ads that help keep the App free to use. To serve and measure ads, Google (and its partners) may automatically collect and process certain information when you use the App, including:

  • Your mobile Advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID / iOS Advertising Identifier).
  • Device and hardware identifiers, operating system version, and app version.
  • IP address, approximate location, and network information.
  • Information about your interaction with advertisements (e.g. ads shown, clicked).

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own policies and the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses data from apps that use AdMob.

Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

AdMob may show personalized ads based on the Advertising ID and other collected data. Where applicable (e.g. in the European Economic Area, the UK, and where required by law), we request consent before personalized advertising is enabled, and we support Google's UMP (User Messaging Platform) for consent management. You can withdraw consent or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings (e.g. "Reset advertising ID" or disabling ad personalization).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the core encrypted messaging features of the App.
  • To display and measure advertisements through AdMob, which financially supports the operation and continued development of the free App.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, and technical problems related to advertising.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared with:

  • Google AdMob — to serve and measure advertisements, as described in Section 2.
  • As required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Data Retention

Encrypted messages, contacts, and keys stored on your device remain there until you delete them. Any data processed by third-party advertising services is retained according to their own retention policies.

6. Security

We take data protection seriously. Messages are encrypted using strong, industry-standard cryptography (ECC P-256 hybrid with AES-256-GCM) on your device. Note that no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Third-Party Links / Services

The App may contain links to or integrate with third-party services (including Google and its advertising partners). Our Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
